Privacy Policy

Application: Bangladesh Airforce Mini

Package (Google Play ID): com.zyeemzia.bangladeshairforcemini

Developer: ZyeemZia™ / ZBZGames™ (Zyeem Bin Zia), Dhaka, Bangladesh

Effective date / Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information Bangladesh Airforce Mini uses, who receives it and the choices you have. Bangladesh Airforce Mini is a single-player, offline arcade flight campaign set during the 1971 Liberation War. It has no accounts and no developer-operated servers.

1. Summary: At A Glance

This summary matches the Data safety declaration for the app on Google Play:

  • Collected and shared: your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general network information, used by Google AdMob to deliver advertising. Not linked to your identity.
  • Stored on your device only: which missions you have unlocked, your best score, star rating and time for each mission, your experience points and rank, which quiz questions you have answered correctly, which archive entries you have read, and your theme, difficulty and sound preferences. Never uploaded.
  • Not collected at all: no name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, camera or microphone data, photos, files, health data or payment details.
  • Permissions: internet access, network state, the advertising ID permission required by the ads SDK, and vibration for haptic feedback on the flight controls. The app requests no runtime permissions at all.
  • Advertising: yes, through Google AdMob. Full-screen advertisements between missions, an advertisement when the app is opened after you have completed a mission, and optional rewarded advertisements. No banner advertisements.
  • Purchases: none. The app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

2. Information the App Uses

The whole game, including all ten missions, the mission archive and the history quiz, runs on your device. You do not need an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts. The app asks only for the permissions it needs to work and to show advertisements.

  • Local game data: your campaign progress, best score, star rating and completion time for each mission, experience points and rank, the quiz questions you have answered correctly, the archive entries you have read, and your theme, difficulty and sound preferences are kept in a single record in the app's private storage on your device. This data is processed on the device and is never uploaded to the cloud or sent to us. If the record is missing or unreadable, the game starts a fresh one instead of failing.
  • Internet and network state: used only to request advertisements and consent messages. The campaign is fully playable without a connection.
  • Vibration: used only to give a short pulse when you press an on-screen control. It reads nothing and sends nothing.
  • Advertising ID: the advertising ID permission is required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which collects device identifiers and the Android advertising identifier to deliver advertisements. The app itself never reads, stores or sends your advertising identifier for any purpose of its own.
  • Location: not used. The game's map is a fictionalised rendering of 1971 East Pakistan drawn entirely in code. The app does not request or access your device's GPS or location.
  • Camera and microphone: not used. The game is played entirely with touch controls on a digital canvas.

The app requests no runtime permissions at all.

3. Third-Party Services

To keep the game free to play, the app uses two components, both provided by Google. Each operates under Google's own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Google AdMob: shows full-screen advertisements between missions, an advertisement when the app is opened after you have already completed a mission, and optional rewarded advertisements. A rewarded advertisement is offered in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as doubling the experience points earned from a sortie; declining one never blocks your progress or affects the campaign. The game does not display banner advertisements. AdMob automatically collects and uses service data, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and network information, to serve relevant advertisements and prevent fraud. All user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Advertisements are restricted to a maximum content rating suitable for teen audiences. Google explains how it handles this data at business.safety.google/privacy, and lists its advertising partners at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): a Google-certified consent management platform, used to ask for and record your advertising choices where the law requires it. It stores your choice on your device so that it applies to future sessions.

There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no social login, no advertising mediation network, no cloud database and no server operated by us.

Bangladesh Airforce Mini does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. All gameplay is free; rewarded advertisements only ever grant optional bonuses.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • No account data: the app has no user accounts, so we hold no personal profile data on any server.
  • Local data: your campaign progress, scores, rank and preferences stay on your device. You can delete all of it at any time through Settings > Apps > Bangladesh Airforce Mini > Storage > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing storage also removes the advertising consent choice recorded on your device, so the consent message will be shown again on the next launch where it applies.
  • No deletion request needed: because we do not collect your data or link your use of the app to a personally identifiable profile on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us (see Contact).
  • Advertising data: data processed by Google for advertising is retained by Google under Google's own policies, linked above.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use behavioural monitoring or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), and the game does not report crashes to us. Google AdMob collects only the data it needs to serve advertisements and maintain network security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Permissions: the app requests no runtime permissions. You can review the app's permissions at any time in your device's Android Settings.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where this applies in your region, a consent message from a consent management platform (CMP) is shown before advertising starts, letting you grant or refuse permission for personalised advertising. You can refuse, and the game remains fully playable.
  • United States: residents of states with applicable privacy laws are shown a notice that lets them opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Changing or withdrawing your choice: you can reset the choice recorded on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage through Settings > Apps > Bangladesh Airforce Mini > Storage > Clear storage. The consent message will then be shown again on the next launch where it applies.
  • Opting out of personalised ads: you can opt out of personalised advertising at any time, independently of this app, in your device's system settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads) or by resetting your advertising ID.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because the app keeps your data on your device, most of these you can exercise directly on the device; for anything else, contact us.

7. Children's Privacy

Bangladesh Airforce Mini is intended for users aged 13 and over (or 16 in certain jurisdictions) and is not directed at children. The app is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme, and advertising is restricted to a maximum content rating suitable for teen audiences.

Please note that the game depicts historical armed conflict: it portrays air operations flown by Kilo Flight during the 1971 Liberation War, in a stylised, non-graphic form.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us so that we can take steps to have it removed from any associated third-party advertising networks.

8. Security

We do not send your progress or gameplay data to any server, so it stays protected by your device's sandboxed app storage. Advertisement and consent requests travel over encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. International Processing

Google, which provides the advertising and consent services named above, may process data on servers outside your country under its own safeguards.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in Google Play Data safety requirements or new app features. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also note them in the app's store listing or release notes.

11. Contact

If you have any questions or requests about this policy or how your data is handled, email ZyeemZia™ at admin@zyeemzia.com. You can also use the Direct Transmission Hub at the bottom of this page.
