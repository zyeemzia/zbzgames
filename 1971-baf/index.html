1971 BAF

Where ZBZGames began. Long before the arcade and the Google Play titles, a young Zyeem Bin Zia built this Windows game in honour of the Bangladesh Air Force and the Liberation War of 1971, and gave it away free with the words This game is not for sale. It is kept here exactly as it shipped in 2004.

Download for Windows Installer · 7.0 MB · Windows · Free

The story

1971 BAF was the very first game published under the ZBZGames™ name, created and published by Zyeem Bin Zia in 2004 while still a child. Its spirit lives on in Bangladesh Airforce Mini on Google Play, a modern ten-mission campaign flying with Kilo Flight, the first air operations unit of the Bangladesh Air Force.

Playing it on Windows 10 or 11

  1. Download and open 1971-BAF-Setup.exe. It is a 2004 installer without a modern signature, so Windows may show Windows protected your PC: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. If the installer asks for a password, the original one from 2004 is godzbzgames.
  3. If the game does not start, right-click its shortcut, choose Properties, then Compatibility, tick Run this program in compatibility mode for Windows XP (Service Pack 3), and try again.
  4. Old DirectX games may also need Microsoft's free DirectX End-User Runtime.
