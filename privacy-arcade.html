Arcade privacy

The ZBZGames™ Arcade at zbzgames.zyeemzia.com/play/ is built to collect nothing. This page covers the browser games, the Kids corner, the installable app and the 1971 BAF download. The Google Play games have their own policies, listed in the Trust & Security Center.

Nothing collected

  • AccountsNone. No sign-up, no login.
  • AdvertisingNone.
  • Analytics and trackingNone. No cookies.
  • Personal dataNone is asked for or sent to us.

What stays on your device

Best scores, your light or dark choice and the sound setting are saved in your own browser so they are there next time. Clearing the site's data, or uninstalling the app, removes them. They are never sent anywhere.

Kids corner

The Kids corner and the Bangla, Arabic and English learning games are made to be safe to hand to a child: no chat, no ads, no purchases, no links out of the arcade and no data collection. Finger tracing in the Write mode is checked on the device itself and never uploaded.

Letters are read aloud by your device's own text-to-speech voice. Some phones may use their maker's online speech service to produce that voice; that is a setting of the device, not of the game, and it can be changed in the phone's text-to-speech settings.

The installable app

Installing ZBZGames keeps a copy of the games in your browser so they open instantly and play offline. When a new version is published the app offers to update; nothing installs without your tap. Uninstalling removes the copy.

1971 BAF download

1971 BAF is the original 2004 Windows installer, offered free and unchanged as a piece of ZBZGames history. It is provided as is, and it runs on your computer, not on our servers. Check its SHA-256 fingerprint on the 1971 BAF page before you run it.
